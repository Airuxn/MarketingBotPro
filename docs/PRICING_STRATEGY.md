## Billing Model: Monthly vs Annual

### Monthly Subscription (Recommended Primary)
**Pros:**
- Lower barrier to entry
- Easier for clients to start
- Predictable monthly revenue
- Industry standard

**Cons:**
- More payment processing fees
- Potential for higher churn

### Annual Subscription (Recommended Secondary)
**Pros:**
- Better cash flow (upfront payment)
- Lower churn (committed for year)
- 17% discount incentivizes annual
- Less payment processing

**Cons:**
- Higher barrier to entry
- Need to offer discount

### Recommendation:
- **Default to Monthly** (easier to start)
- **Offer Annual with 17% discount** (2 months free)
- **Show savings clearly** on pricing page
