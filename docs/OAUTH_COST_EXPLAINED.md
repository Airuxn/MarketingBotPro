# OAuth Cost Explained - IT'S FREE! 🎉

## Quick Answer: **NO PAYMENT REQUIRED**

OAuth login is **100% FREE** for all platforms:
- ✅ Facebook - FREE
- ✅ Twitter/X - FREE  
- ✅ LinkedIn - FREE
- ✅ Instagram - FREE (uses Facebook)

## What You Need to Do

1. **Create a FREE developer account** with each platform
2. **Register your app** (takes 5-10 minutes per platform)
3. **Get your credentials** (App ID and Secret - both FREE)
4. **That's it!** Your clients can then log in forever

## Why Is It Free?

Social media platforms WANT you to use their login system because:
- It makes it easier for users to sign up (they already have accounts)
- Users stay in their ecosystem
- It's good for their platform

So they provide OAuth **completely free** - no strings attached!

## What About "App Review" or "Live Mode"?

- **Development Mode**: Works immediately, FREE - you and test users can log in
- **Live Mode**: Also FREE - just requires Facebook to review your app (they verify you're not spam)
- **App Review**: FREE process - they just check that your app is legitimate

**You don't pay for any of this!**

## Comparison to Manual Tokens

- **OAuth (FREE)**: You set up once, clients just log in ✅
- **Manual Tokens (FREE but harder)**: Each client must get their own token from Facebook Developer Console ❌

OAuth is FREE AND easier for everyone!

## Summary

✅ **FREE to set up**  
✅ **FREE to use**  
✅ **FREE forever**  
✅ **Clients don't pay anything**  
✅ **You don't pay anything**

Just create developer accounts (which are free) and register your app. That's it!
