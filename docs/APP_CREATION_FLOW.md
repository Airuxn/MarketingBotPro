# Facebook App Creation Flow - Step by Step

## Current Step: "Add use cases"

You're at the "Add use cases" step. Here's what to do:

### Step 1: Select Use Cases (or Skip)

On the "Add use cases" page:

**Option A: Select a use case**
- Click "Content management (5)" filter on the left
- OR select any use case that seems relevant
- Click the checkbox next to a use case
- Click "Next" or "Continue"

**Option B: Skip use cases (if possible)**
- Look for "Skip" or "Skip for now" button
- OR just select something general like "Create & manage ads"
- Click "Next" or "Continue"

### Step 2: Complete App Creation

After selecting use cases (or skipping):
1. Fill in any remaining required fields:
   - App name (you probably already did this)
   - Contact email
   - App icon (optional - can add later)
   - Category (if asked)
2. Click "Create App" or "Finish" or "Next"

### Step 3: Access Your App Dashboard

After app is created:
1. You'll be taken to your app dashboard
2. You'll see the app overview/main page
3. **NOW you'll see "Add Product"** option

### Step 4: Add Facebook Login Product

Once in your app dashboard:
1. Look for **"Add Product"** section (usually on the left sidebar or main area)
2. OR go to "Products" section
3. Find **"Facebook Login"** 
4. Click **"Set Up"** or **"Get Started"**

## If You Still Don't See "Add Product"

1. **Check if app creation is complete:**
   - Are you still in the creation wizard?
   - Did you click "Create App" or "Finish"?
   - Are you on the app dashboard/home page?

2. **Look in different places:**
   - Left sidebar - might be called "Products" or "Tools"
   - Main dashboard area - might be a card or button
   - Top navigation - might be in a dropdown

3. **Take a screenshot:**
   - What page are you on?
   - What options do you see?
   - Share a screenshot if possible

## Alternative: Quickstart

Some apps have a "Quickstart" option:
1. Look for "Quickstart" or "Getting Started" in left sidebar
2. Click it
3. It might guide you through adding Facebook Login

Let me know what page you're on or what options you see!
